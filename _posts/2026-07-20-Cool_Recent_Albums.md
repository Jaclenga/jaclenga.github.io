# Cool Stuff I Listened to 7/12 - 7/19
I got kinda bored, so I just wanted to show off some of the music I've been listening to. Here are a few albums that I really liked.

## Black Celebration - Depeche Mode
![Black Celebration Album cover](https://i.scdn.co/image/ab67616d0000b2732b9394d33dfa48289152c815)

When I hear people talk about Depeche Mode, it's usually about their other albums. In my opinion, *Black Celebration* is among the band's best. *Stripped* and *A Question of Lust* are among my favorite songs of all time.

## Imaginal Disk - Magdalena Bay

![Imaginal Disk Cover](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/4/4b/Magdalena_Bay_-_Imaginal_Disk.png)

I didn't expect to love this album so much. The run from *Image* to *Death & Romance* to *Fear, Sex* was a fantastic experience.

## Violator - Depeche Mode

![Depeche Mode Violator](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/9/92/Depeche_Mode_-_Violator.png)

Since I really enjoyed *Black Celebration*, I decided to just listen to the entire *Violator* album again. Still holds up. Ig this time around I appreciate *Halo* a little more too.
